FAQ

Things riders ask us

Quick answers on what we service, certifications, hours and booking. Anything else — call 905‑576‑1666.

Do you only work on Harley‑Davidsons?

No. Pushrod specializes in Harley‑Davidson performance, but we service all makes and models of American and imported motorcycles — tires, brakes, oil changes, fuel systems, diagnostics and safety inspections.

Do you do Ontario motorcycle safety inspections?

Yes. Pushrod Performance Cycle is a licensed Motor Vehicle Inspection Station, so we can issue the safety standards certificate you need to plate a bike or transfer ownership in Ontario.

What does S&S Pro Tune Certified mean?

S&S Cycle trains and certifies technicians to install and tune its high‑performance engine kits — big‑bore cylinders, cams and complete engines for Harley‑Davidson and similar V‑twins. Being certified means your S&S kit is installed and tuned the way the manufacturer intended.

Can you rebuild or tune a carburetor?

Yes. We service and rebuild most carburetors, including synchronizing multi‑carburetor setups, and we diagnose and service fuel‑injected bikes as well.

When should I book an engine build or restoration?

Ideally in the fall. Engine builds, S&S kit installs, rebuilds and classic restorations take weeks, so most riders drop the bike off between October and February and have it ready for spring. Slots are booked in order, so earlier is better.

Do you handle insurance repairs?

Yes. We file claims and complete the repairs.

Where are you located and when are you open?

4‑199 Waterloo St in Oshawa, Ontario, with parking on site. Open Tuesday to Friday 9 am–5 pm and most Saturdays 10 am–2 pm — call 905‑576‑1666 to confirm a Saturday. We serve Durham Region, Peterborough, Richmond Hill and the GTA.

Do you sell parts and gift certificates?

Yes. We stock and order performance and OEM parts from brands including S&S, ThunderMax, Zipper's, Drag Specialties and Kuryakyn, and gift certificates are available.
